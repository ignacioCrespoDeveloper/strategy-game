⚔ Army Matchups🏴 Raiding Guide

Expeditions pull in two directions at once. Expedition Rating is a floor pushing your army bigger — it gates both the quality of what you find and which recruits you attract, and scouts count double toward it. Footprint is a ceiling pushing back — past 600 PWR you get ambushed more, come back empty more, and the quiet finds stay hidden. Past 800 PWR an ambush fields more power than you brought. And a recruit that won't fit under your army cap is lost outright, not queued.

So the answer is never "bring everything" — it is a scout-heavy force at roughly half your cap, with a small line escort to screen the lord. A pure-scout party loses its lord about 43% of the time it's ambushed; adding a couple of line troops drops that to ~4%.

ER = Σ unitPWR × (scout ? 2 : 1)
loudness = clamp((armyPWR − 600) / 400, 0, 1)
ambush = 0.90 below 800 PWR, rising to 1.30 at 1100 PWR
army cap = 200 + 80 × lordLevel  (max 1100 with the Commanding talent)

Find tierEvery discovery carries a loot tier 1–3, which decides its payout band. ER decides which tiers you are offered at all — the roll draws the tier from your band first, then picks a find of that tier that suits the terrain. A Common-rated force is offered tier 1 only; Legendary is offered tier 3 on 25% of its finds.
PWRA unit's combat power (EconomyCore.getUnitPower). Your army's total PWR is capped at 200 + 80 × lordLevel.
HeadroomCap − army PWR. Free capacity to receive a recruit. A recruit that doesn't fit is lost outright, not queued.
LoudnessHow conspicuous the army is: 0 below 600 PWR, rising to 1.0 at 1000. Higher means more ambushes, more empty expeditions, and smaller find rewards.
FootprintThe umbrella name for the loudness penalties — the ceiling that stops "bring everything" from winning.
OvermatchPast 800 PWR an ambush fields more power than you brought, up to 130% at 1100 PWR.

Tiers unlock — they are not merely weighted. A Common force (ER under 200) finds tier 1 and nothing else: tier 2 and tier 3 are closed to it outright. Reaching Uncommon opens tier 2 and a 1.0% sliver of tier 3; Rare widens tier 3 to 5.0%; Legendary takes it to 25% and all but retires tier 1.

Why gate it rather than weight it? A tier-3 find pays 25–50k resources before scaling. Handed to a level-2 lord that is roughly twenty times their whole economy — it doesn't reward the player, it erases the early game. In the other direction a tier-1 find pays 800–1500, which at endgame is rounding error and reads as a wasted expedition. So the tiers phase in and out, and Expedition Rating is the only thing that moves you between them.

Two details worth knowing. The recruit pool is hard-limited to your band as well — a unit either walks into your camp or it doesn't. And terrain no longer decides how good a find is, only which find of that tier you get: ER draws the tier first, then terrain picks from it, so a marsh still can't hand you a marble quarry. Percentages are for plains at loudness 0 and are shares of finds — expeditions that come back empty, get ambushed or attract recruits are excluded, since those aren't finds.

The same rating, applied twice. ER used to gate recruits and nothing else, so what you found was pure lottery — a naked level-1 lord had the same odds of a tier-3 vault as an 800-ER scout column. Now the band that decides who joins you also decides how rich the ground is. Multipliers stack on top of terrain, so ER buys better odds on ground that could have held the find — it never conjures a marble quarry out of a marsh.

Short is the most gold-efficient per hour (0.6× reward for ⅓ the time), but Long is the only realistic way to see Legendary finds. Scout-heavy parties should favour Short — the ambush rate on Long is where their lord gets killed.
